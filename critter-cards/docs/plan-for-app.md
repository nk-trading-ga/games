# Plan for Critter Cards

## What is it?

A creature-card battle game for a child on an iPad. You have cards; at the
start you choose one of five, and you battle other trainers with it. To attack,
the critter comes out of its card and uses its own special abilities.

All the critters are our own — none of them are from any other game.

## How do you play?

Pick a card. Each battle, both cards float up and the critters pop out. Tap one
of three big buttons: a bump, a special move, or a super move once it has
charged. The critter whose puff runs out goes back into its card. Beat the
four other trainers to be Champion.

## What does it look like?

A sunny meadow with a round lawn to battle on, trees and hills round it. The
critters are chunky and toon-shaded with dark outlines. The cards look like
trading cards: the critter's picture, its kind, a line about it and its moves.

## What's in the first version?

- Five critters, five kinds, three moves each, each special with its own effect.
- Four computer trainers in a row, getting a little harder.
- Gentle losing: try the same battle again.
- Sound, made in code; a sound switch.

## Notes

- "Attack other people" is computer trainers for now: the site is static, with
  no server to meet other players on. Two players taking turns on one iPad
  would be a natural next step.
- More critters, and collecting new cards by winning, could come later.
